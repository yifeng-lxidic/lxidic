About this exploration

The signal view describes changes from a personal baseline. The v1.1 research model below uses age, cohort sex coding and optional earlier measurements. Wearable inputs currently have zero prediction weight.

The brain is a shared cortex template. Its colors are a visual response to fictional inputs, rather than a map of brain activity or amyloid.

What the latest model can estimatev1.1 · Research example

Explore a fictional adult, with or without an earlier measured reference. In the current dataset, wearable features did not improve prediction beyond age and cohort sex coding. Their prediction weight is zero, so the signal sliders above do not change these estimates.

Literature model of PET positivity—

Intervals show the model's 80% residual range. In this small, approximately five-week dataset, most plasma predictability came from the earlier blood measurement; calibration did not clearly improve on carrying that value forward. EDA was unavailable. The cohort used a wrist device, not this ring.

The PET model is a literature-based population calculation for cognitively unimpaired adults. This cohort has no paired PET validation. Plasma concentrations, PET positivity and brain tissue concentration are distinct endpoints; no brain Aβ concentration is measured or inferred here. All examples are fictional and run locally, with no data submission.

Sources: OpenNeuro ds003823 · Jansen et al., 2015 · A4 screening, 2020. Recent sensor context: wearable validation, November 2026. Newer publication dates alone do not validate this model.

Methods, evidence & sources

The sleep reference

Sleep uses a circular median of at least 14 comparable nights in the preceding 28 calendar nights. Timing shifts take the shortest clock distance. Regularity is the median absolute midpoint change between observed consecutive nights, shown at 70% pair coverage. These are engineering rules retained in v1.1.

Physiology & the cortex

Recovery and skin arousal are experimental peripheral physiology indices. They use personal-history median and median absolute deviation, with 50 as the reference. Skin temperature shows a standardized deviation. These indices do not feed the v1.1 clinical-target predictions.
